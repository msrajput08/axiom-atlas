How Electric Field & Coulomb’s Law Calculator Works

1 Overview

Calculate electric field strength E = k|q|/r² and electrostatic Coulomb force F = kq₁q₂/r². Designed for physics & engineering (electromagnetism), this tool evaluates your inputs in canonical SI or domain units and generates a verified step-by-step derivation.

2 Formula

E = k_e |q₁| / r²; F = k_e q₁ q₂ / r² (k_e = 8.98755 × 10⁹ N·m²/C²)
