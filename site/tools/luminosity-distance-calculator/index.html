How Luminosity Distance Calculator Works

1 Overview

Compute cosmological luminosity distance D_L(z) = (1+z)D_M(z), distance modulus μ(z), and flux dilution via numerical ΛCDM integration. Designed for astronomy & astrophysics (cosmology), this tool evaluates your inputs in canonical SI or domain units and generates a verified step-by-step derivation.

2 Formula

D_L(z) = (1 + z) D_M(z); μ(z) = 5 log₁₀(D_L / 10 pc)

6 Assumptions & Limitations

  • Accounts for both photon energy redshift (1+z) and time-dilation arrival-rate reduction (1+z), plus spatial curvature Ω_k.
  • All internal evaluations use SI units and IAU 2012/2015 & CODATA 2018 physical constants (c = 299,792,458 m/s, G = 6.67430 × 10⁻¹¹ m³·kg⁻¹·s⁻², 1 AU = 149,597,870,700 m).
  • Cosmological distances and lookback times assume a homogeneous, isotropic FLRW metric; simple linear Hubble approximations (v = H₀d) are valid only at low redshift (z ≪ 0.1).
